Accounting & P&L Definitions:
• Starting Allocation: Initial paper bankroll ($400 per model = 20 traders × $20).
• Current Balance (Equity): Current available cash + mark-to-market value of open positions (recycled capital).
• Realized P&L: Cumulative profit/loss locked in upon position exits (sales or market resolutions).
• Unrealized P&L: Mark-to-market gain/loss on currently active open positions (open_value − open_stake). If mark prices have not yet fluctuated or are not in the export snapshot, reported as $0.00 or N/A.
• Why Realized P&L can differ from Balance Change: When Model B or D exits a trade at profit, proceeds are returned to that trader's cash pool and immediately recycled into subsequent $2 copy trades. The cumulative Realized P&L sums all historical gains, while Current Balance reflects active equity after subsequent position deployments and marks.
